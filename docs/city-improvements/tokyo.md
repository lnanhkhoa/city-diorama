- **Fixed 2026-10-21, second pass:**

  - Senso-ji re-laid along X: `kaminarimon` (scale 0.85, ry pi/2) at (4.95, -6.9), two rows of three stalls at z -6.0 / -7.8 with red lantern strings between them, a jokoro incense burner at (7.4, -6.9), `mainHall` at (8.95, -6.9), pagoda (scale 0.8) at (7.0, -8.95), side shrine at (4.9, -9.0), new gravel lane patch.

  - Shibuya: all static people removed from the carriageways (six now stand on the corner pavements); three pilgrims walk the Nakamise lane and three strollers loop through the park (`anim.follow`, closed paths).
  - Night: Skytree (`SKY_WHITE`, blue-white) and Tokyo Tower (orange / warm white) are `floodMat` (plain by day, lit at night); `lampMaterial` beacons on both tips; Kaminarimon lantern, lantern strings and hall lanterns use a `floodMat` red `LANTERN`; hall windows use `windowMaterial`.
- **Left open:** the road bridge is still at water level and boats turn back instead of passing; Shibuya 109 / Hachiko plaza, Asahi flame, tall glass towers, station canopies and a longer train from the audit are untouched; pedestrians do not cross the scramble because the cars there are not signal-synchronised.

## Audit

Original findings, written before the 2026-10-21 fix pass; line numbers refer to the code at that date. Reference: none (compared with the real landmarks). Budget at audit time: 40220 tris, 146 draws (headroom ~49k tris, ~74 draws).

Observed in day + night screenshots (default view and 3x wheel zoom); no console errors.

### Bugs
- **Station boxes z-fight on the viaduct** (`build()` l.368-372 vs l.359): station box z-extent is `deckZ ± 0.65`; the 19.6-long parapet strips (`box(19.6, .1, .06)` at `deckZ ± 0.62`, l.359) have their outer face at exactly `deckZ ± 0.65`, same height range (y 1.24-1.34) -> coplanar faces. Seen as black dashed speckle along the lower edge of the right-hand station wall. Fix: shorten the parapets to `x in [-6.9, 6.9]` (stop at the station boxes) or make the station `d` 1.34. The dark `box(.03,.5,1.0)` at x=±6.9 (l.371) also sits half-embedded in the box's end face; push it out to ±6.88.
- **Kaminarimon ridge bar sticks out of the roof** (`kaminarimon()` l.174-175): `box(2.5,.07,.12)` at y 2.6 is longer than the ridge of `hipRoof(3.3,1.7,.7)` (ridge ~1.6 long) so it floats as a dark pipe over both hips (clearly visible in the zoom shot). Shorten to ~1.5 or drop it; the two 0.14 cubes at ±1.25 (l.175) then also float in air - remove/move to ±0.75.
- **Boats pass through the bridge** (l.280 road at x=3 across the river, l.391-392): the road slab (top y 0.05) is one solid piece over water (top 0.04), so both boats (y .03) sail through the asphalt at x=3. Either give the bridge a deck above `y ≈ 0.25` with piers/rail posts (and re-height the cars on it, l.387-388 use y .05), or stop boats short of the bridge (reverse path at x≈1.8 / 4.2).
- **River kerbs cut through the road at the bridge** (l.275-276): concrete kerb strips are `h 0.08` (top .08) but `road()` top is .05, so they show as white bars across the carriageway at z=-3.19/-4.81 (visible in zoom). Intended as bridge parapets but they block/clip the lane; split the kerb strips to leave a gap at x∈[2.1,3.9].
- **Blossom row embedded in buildings** (l.354): `row(-9.4,6.9 → 1.4,6.9, 6)` puts trees at x≈-9.4, -5.1, -0.8 exactly on the z≈6.9 front faces of blocks `[-8.6,8.1,…]`, `[-4.2,8.1,…]`, `[0.4,8.1,…]` (l.323; those start at z 6.9). A pink canopy pokes out of the corner at the cross street. Move row to z=6.75 or skip x inside block spans.
- **Over the plinth limit**: `fuji()` at x=z=-7.8 with r=2.1 (l.111, l.334) reaches -9.9 (limit -9.8); `mainHall()` roof `hipRoof(3.0,1.7)` (l.150) at z=-9 reaches z=-9.85. Nudge `fuji` to -7.6 / hall to -8.9.
- **Skytree foot pads are mis-centred** (`skytree()` l.55): `x: 1.35*cos(a) - 0.13 + 0.13*0` - leftover arithmetic offsets every pad 0.13 off its leg. Drop the `-0.13 + 0.13*0`.
- **Pedestrians and cars share the Shibuya scramble** (l.297-301 static people vs taxis on l.385-388 lanes z=5.15/5.85 and x=2.65/3.35): cars drive straight through 30+ standing people (zoom shot). Either stop cars at the crossing (use staggered `anim.follow` delays or shorten paths) or make the crowd move (see Missing).
- Hall sub-roof overlap: side shrine (l.340-341, x 4.3-5.4) overlaps the main hall roof footprint (x≥5.0, l.150) and `pagoda` base (x 7.8-9.8) overlaps hall roof at x 7.8-8.0 / z -8.4..-8.15; move pagoda to x=9.0 or hall to x=6.2 [unverified visually].
- Crossing paint at y .05-.062 sits only .007 above the intersection patch (`patch(... h .055)`, l.282; zebra l.287, diagonals l.295 at y .058) - may shimmer at far zoom [unverified]; raise patch to h .05 (same as `road()`) or lift paint.

### Landmark fidelity
- **Mt Fuji** (`fuji()` l.109-116, placed l.334): 28-seg straight cone + a smaller cone cap; reads as a party hat, only 3.4 tall so it is dwarfed and half-hidden behind the block at (-5.6,-5.7) (l.324). Use `lathe` with a concave profile (steeper near the summit, flared foot), a truncated crater top, and a jagged snow skirt (6-8 snow wedges/cones of different length down the slope); raise to ~4.5 and delete/lower block `[-5.6,-5.7,...]` so it shows from the camera.
- **Skytree** (`skytree()` l.49-82): slender and correct in structure (tripod base to round shaft, two decks, gain mast) but base radius 1.35 on a 13.5-unit tower is too thin (real ratio ~1:6 at foot, here 1:10) and goes above the 13-unit cap; widen base to r≈1.8 with a more clearly triangular footprint, lower to ~12.8 by shortening the antenna (l.80). Add emissive blue/white at night (see below).
- **Tokyo Tower** (`tokyoTower()` l.85-107): orange/white banding and main/top decks read well; gaps: the real tower has a flared, almost arched foot and the main deck is a two-storey block with a visible 'Main Deck' band and a lower 'foot-town' building; here the 4 legs are just stacked straight lattice and the footing cubes (l.92) are tiny. Add a low `building` (0.9 high, 1.6 wide) between the legs and make the lowest segment flare to r≈1.9. Also it is 9.5 high vs Skytree 13.5: real ratio 333:634; keep it, but it is lost behind the block at (-8.4,3.8).
- **Kaminarimon** (`kaminarimon()` l.156-187): good bones (pillars, nuki, lantern, double roof), but the iconic lantern is only ~0.5 high (≈25px on screen) and half-hidden under the eave, the Fujin/Raijin statues are 0.3x0.55 red boxes (l.185) and the roof is a plain hip with no turned-up ridge ends. Scale the lantern ×1.5 with black base/top rings and a white kanji panel on the visible face (z+), add the two flanking guardian niches as dark openings with ochre figures, give the roof `profile()`-extruded curved eaves (like the pagoda) instead of flat `hipRoof` pair.
- **Sensō-ji hall / layout** (`mainHall()` l.145-154, placed l.337-345): gate at z=-5.5 and hall at z=-9.0 are only ~3.5 apart and the gate's roof hides the whole Nakamise lane (stalls at x=5.55/7.45, z=-6.5..-7.9, l.343-344 are behind the gate from the camera). Hall is a generic two-tier box. Rotate the approach to run along X (gate at left end, stalls row visible, hall at far right), add the Hōzōmon, an incense burner (jōkōro) and lanterns hanging between stalls; give the hall an irimoya roof with curved ridge and a wide veranda.
- **Pagoda** (`pagoda()` l.119-143): best-built object; eaves are flat boxes with a frustum rim, no upturned corners (the doc comment says upturned, geometry is not). Add four small corner `cone`/`beam` kicks per tier, or `profile()` curved eaves; fine otherwise.
- **Shibuya crossing / buildings** (`build()` l.279-317): the scramble itself is convincing (diagonals, zebras, crowd); the corner buildings are plain boxes with 4 screens each. Add a Hachiko statue + small plaza, a Shibuya 109 cylinder with the red '109' sign (now a stripy `tower round` l.315 with 2 orange/purple panels - reads as a generic silo), and a Shibuya Scramble Square-style tall glass tower behind.
- **Shinkansen** (`shinkansen()` l.198-216): nose profile is good; 4 cars at ~0.3 wide are OK at this scale; add blue lower stripe nose and a red/blue pantograph, or at least longer train (6 cars) because it disappears into the station boxes (l.368) after 1 s.
- **Station boxes** (l.368-372): blank grey boxes with black slab roof: add platform canopy, windows/sign, and an actual opening where tracks enter; currently they look like warehouses, and the left one hides Tokyo Tower's base.
- **Asahi hall** (l.328-329): golden `building` + white box on top; the real Asahi Beer Hall has the golden 'flame' (Flamme d'Or) on a black building; swap the white cube (l.329) for a lathe/profile teardrop in gold and make the body black/dark.

### Missing / sparse
- Almost every block is the same `midrise()` (l.218-252): light grey `WALLS`, grid windows, neon panel, blade sign. Heights 1.4-3.5 only; no skyscrapers (Shinjuku/Marunouchi), no pencil buildings, no dark glass or brick, no low wooden machiya in the Asahi area. Add 3-4 tall `tower({ sections: [...] })` glass towers (6-8 units, `windows: 'bands'`) at the back-left corner (x -9..-6, z -9..-6) behind Fuji? or at left edge, plus 2 low old houses with `gableRoof` around Senso-ji.
- No Rainbow Bridge/bay, Tokyo Station, Imperial Palace moat, torii, Tokyo Dome - only 3 true landmarks + temple + Shibuya. Cheapest high-recognition: a red torii (3 boxes) at the temple entrance and a rainbow-bridge-style suspension span replacing the viaduct end ([unverified] feasibility vs. river axis).
- Back-left pavement behind the left blocks (x -9.5..-6, z -9.5..-5.5, around Fuji) and the strip between the river and the avenue (z -3.2..0.3) are empty slabs with a few trees and the viaduct; add vending machines, bike racks, a small park/shrine, street trees on the avenue (only lamps on l.379-380).
- Street life: only people are static `person()` meshes (l.297-301, 346, 353); cars: 6 on the cross street, 8 on the avenue, all but a few taxis; boats 2. Add: pedestrians via `anim.follow` crossing the scramble in waves, cyclists, a second train on the left side, buses (`bus()` unused), a yakatabune boat with lanterns on the river, birds/ koi-nobori? Also a Yamanote-line-style rail above the cross street.
- Cherry blossom trees are only 8 in the park + 10 on the river bank at s=0.8: expand along the Sumida bank (Sumida Park is the signature sakura spot) and add petals; they are the main Tokyo seasonal accent.
- Night look (screenshot): windows glow and neon panels read well, but the three towers/temple do not: `skytree()` decks use `windowMaterial` (small) while the lattice stays dull grey; Tokyo Tower orange is not lit (it is in reality); the pagoda/gate have no glow except the lantern (constant `emissive 0.4` even by day, l.158). Add emissive materials: Skytree blue/white (`mat(0x6fd4ff,{emissive,...})` on rings), Tokyo Tower orange emissive lattice, lantern strings (lampMaterial) along the Nakamise, shrine hall windows via `windowMaterial`.
- No lighting on the viaduct/trains at night; train windows use `windowMaterial` (good), but station boxes are dark.

### Rodin candidates
- **Mt Fuji** (`fuji()`): only borderline candidate (≈180px wide on screen). Prefer a procedural `lathe` profile with a jagged snow skirt (cheap, matches palette) first; use Rodin only if a ridged/eroded slope is wanted.
- Nothing else qualifies: Skytree/Tokyo Tower/pagoda/gate are lattices or architecture (procedural); the guardian statues and lantern are <60px on screen.
