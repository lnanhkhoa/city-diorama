# Ho Chi Minh City improvements

Source: `src/cities/list/ho-chi-minh.ts`. Part of the [city improvements](README.md) audit.

## Status

- **Budget now:** 129,982 tris, 162 draws (limits 200,000 / 300), measured 2026-10-23 after Bitexco was rebuilt procedurally (8,370 tris with its podium and roof gardens, against the 3,000-triangle bake it replaced), the kit tree rework (28 palms ~23k, 51 round trees ~32k) and the move of the floor lines into the towers' glass shaders. The motorbikes take about 33k tris (48 riders on `motorbike-lite`, 696 tris each). Pedestrians cost ~208 tris each since the `person()` rework; 43 are placed.
- **Built 2026-10-21 from `assets/ho-chi-minh.png`:** positions read through a homography fitted to the poster's plinth corners, streets, river and plazas traced on a rectified top-down copy of its ground plane. The Saigon River runs along the left edge and stays inside the plinth: the water stops behind a stone curb along the plinth's top edge (`RIM`), so the plinth sides show no water. The poster's water-in-section sides were dropped at the user's request.
  - **Ba Son bridge:** built as the real bridge rather than the poster's two A-frame pylons: one arch-shaped pylon in the river by the far bank, leaning back away from the main span, 14 stays fanned to both deck edges and 6 backstays. The deck lands on the city bank and ramps down behind the Independence Palace; traffic on the bridge follows the deck height and pitch (`onDeck`). Pylon, stays and parapet strips are lit at night, like the real bridge's 2024 "harp of light".
  - **Landmark 81:** since 2026-10-23 the Rodin shape `landmark-81` (5,000 tris), baked from the 20k Blender reduction of the Rodin download (`scripts/rodin/decimate.py`): nine bundled square tubes stepping up to the centre, crown and needle spire on a stepped podium. Height 12.4 like the procedural tower it replaced; Rodin's model is stockier than the real one, so `xzScale` 0.7 narrows the footprint to the old one (tubes ~1.5 wide, podium ~2.3 at ground), and the base plate plus the lowest podium tier (bottom 0.04 of the bake) are sunk below ground. The colour split smeared frames into the glass, so the whole shape takes `L81_GLASS`, whose shader paints the floor lines (see below). Aviation light on the spire tip and the four entrance flags kept; the entrance canopy is gone (the bake carries the podium).
  - **Landmark 81's crown (2026-10-23, at the user's request, from a dusk photograph):** the crown and needle spire, everything above 0.79 of the bake (the central tube's last setback), take their own colour through `floorLinesGlass`'s `crown`: a pale metal frame (#c9d3db) by day, lit deep red (#e8101e, peak 1.1) at night like the real tower's LED crown, with no floor lines. At night the crown's own colour goes dark so the red reads deep rather than pink.
  - **Landmark 81's LED strips (2026-10-24, at the user's request, from the same photograph):** thin strips (`L81_LED`, grey #8f9aa3 by day, floodlit red #e8101e at night like the crown) run up the tube corners from about half height to the crown: all four corners of the central tube from its last setback (0.68 of the bake), and five convex corners of the outer tubes between 0.45 and 0.67. Each sits on a corner edge found in the bake (two faces meeting sharply), so none floats off the stepped tubes. Nine boxes in one shared material.
  - **Bitexco (procedural again since 2026-10-23, at the user's request: the Rodin bake's mesh was faulty, its glass lumpy and faceted, the helipad a flat blob):** built from photographs of the real tower, drawn a quarter wider as the poster has it, 8.2 tall like the bake and turned -π/4 so the helipad points at the river. The shaft is one smooth loft of elliptical sections (64 round, 30 rows) that swells at a third of its height and tapers; above the swell its south side recedes faster than the north, so the bud leans back to a crown cut 40° away from the helipad, a glass roof inside a silver lip, with the aviation light on the peak. A glass drum leans out of the shaft's south side from about half height and carries the helipad (52nd floor: 5.97 of 8.2) on a dark cantilever dish. A dark joint traces the petal seam where the drum leaves the shaft (a U solved from the two surfaces), and a silver fin climbs the bud tip's edge from the deck to the crown. The deck has a silver parapet with landing lights (lit at night), and a touchdown circle and an H on the part clear of the tower. Dark mechanical bands ring the shaft at 42% and under the helipad. Icon 68, the five-storey podium, is two tiers of curved white walls (floodlit at night) with ribbon windows, roof gardens with clipped shrubs, and glass doors under a canopy toward the forecourt's opening. Four materials of its own (glass, silver frame, dark bands, podium wall), 2 draws more than the bake. It replaced the Rodin shape `bitexco` (2026-10-22, see Rodin below), which had replaced the first procedural tower (commit `71a032f`).
  - **Bitexco's colours (2026-10-23, at the user's request, matched to their daylight photograph):** blue-green glass (#5296a3) with dark blue-green floor lines (#2f6871, through `floorLinesGlass`'s `line`), silver-grey frames (#a9b4b8), and a near-black grey-green dish, collar, bands and petal seam (#2f3d42); the podium stays white. In the app's clear-day light the lit and shaded faces render at hue 184° and 188° (value 0.75 and 0.61) against 186° and 190° (0.67 and 0.64) on the photograph's sunlit and shaded sides: within 2° of hue and 0.03 of mean value, with the diorama's key light giving more contrast between the faces. The pale steel blue (#9db4c9) it replaces came from the poster.
  - **Bitexco at night (2026-10-23):** the blue glow is a faint wash (peak 0.16, glow #3d6fd6, was 0.55 with #6fa8ff). The strong glow flattened the smooth-shaded tower into one waxy pale-blue blob; the dimmer one keeps its shading. Over the blue-green glass the wash reads deep blue, with the lit floor lines warm.
  - **Floor lines (2026-10-23, moved into the glass shaders and thinned at the user's request):** both towers' glass is `floorLinesGlass`. Its fragment shader paints window-glass lines across the walls (flat decks, roofs and the helipad deck stay plain), blue-grey glazing by day on Landmark 81 and dark blue-green on Bitexco, and lit floors at night with `setCityLights`, edges softened over a pixel. They follow the Landmark 81 tubes and Bitexco's lotus taper, drum and bud tip. No geometry: the `shapeBands` slabs they replaced cost 12,662 tris (Landmark 81 8,722, Bitexco 3,940). One line every 0.068 of the tower's height, twice the old spacing: Landmark 81 has 12 (0.055–0.803, width 0.01), Bitexco 13 (0.116–0.932, from just above the podium to under the crown, width 0.008). The shader reads heights in the mesh's own frame, so the towers' glass stays out of static merging: Landmark 81's two bake parts draw separately, and Bitexco's shaft, drum and crown roof are one mesh.
  - **Bitexco's forecourt (2026-10-22, at the user's request: the houses around Bitexco cluttered the view):** the Rodin base is a diamond reaching 1.73 from the centre, much wider than the procedural podium the neighbours were laid out for. The apartment block that touched its corner and the taller shophouse row that stood 0.5 in front of it are gone, and the trees, which avoided the old podium box, grew into the base. Bitexco now stands alone on a round paved forecourt (`BITEXCO_PLAZA_R` 1.98, white curb) that opens onto the street past the cathedral, ringed by clipped shrubs with three people at the opening. Trees keep 0.35 beyond the curb. The two remaining apartment blocks stand along the right edge, the second moved 0.2 toward it; the ground between the shop row and the forecourt is lawn. The dropped row still draws its random numbers, so every tree, crowd and pack elsewhere keeps its place. From the default camera the podium is now in view. The procedural podium (2026-10-23) reaches 1.6 from the centre, its entrance canopy 1.8 in the opening between the people, so the forecourt stays as laid out.
  - **Independence Palace:** the stone-bamboo fin screen on every front, pilotis, roof canopy, rooftop pavilion with the flag, and the helicopter. Its lawn and curved drive face the oval park, whose fountain and flag sit inside a traffic ring.
  - **Notre-Dame Cathedral, Opera House, Central Post Office, Ben Thanh Market:** brick bell towers with belfries, grey spires, rose windows, transept and apse; a mansard hall with the great entrance arch, paired columns and statues; yellow wings of arched windows around the clock gable; arcaded halls, corner pavilions and the three-faced clock tower over the red name band. All floodlit at night.
  - **Streets:** one row of shophouses along the street, two apartment blocks at the right edge, the night-market stalls under a lantern string, the palm plaza monument, palms along the avenues and the promenade.
  - **Ben Thanh square and crossroads (today's layout, at the user's choice):** the poster's roundabout is gone, as the real one has been since 2017, when it made way for the Ben Thanh metro station. The avenue (1.6 wide, narrowed from 2.2 to make room) runs straight past a granite-paved square in front of the south gate: lawns either side of the central walk with small shade trees and flower beds, Tran Nguyen Han on horseback (bronze, on a stepped granite pedestal) back on his old spot facing the gate, and the white Quach Thi Trang bust among the trees on the east lawn, as the 2025–2026 renovation lays it out. The junction at the market's front-west corner (the avenue and the street past the cathedral) is a signalised crossroads: a light on each corner, red for the avenue and green for the cross street, crosswalks on all four arms, and a crossing from the square on the gate's axis.
  - **Traffic:** each pack is one mover, a car or taxi with scooters or a swarm of up to six scooters, on lanes that keep right: closed loops round the oval park, Ben Thanh and the Landmark 81 block, and open paths that end a pack's length inside the plinth edge. The avenue's lanes are westbound 7.8 (curb, the Ben Thanh loop) and 8.15, eastbound 8.8, all clear of the square. Riders wear helmets (the baked hat region painted in helmet colours); shirts and scooter bodies come from one palette, so a pack costs 5–7 draws. A cargo ship and a waterbus run the river; a dinner cruise and sampans lie at the promenade.
  - **Flags:** the seven flags (oval park, palace roof, Ben Thanh's clock tower, four at the Landmark 81 entrance) use the kit's `vietnamFlag`, the same flag as Hanoi: the official red (#da251d) 2:3 field and a centred five-pointed yellow (#ffcd00) star whose points lie on a circle of radius 1/5 of the flag's length. They replace the earlier local flag, which had the shared palette's muted red, a metallic gold star shaped as a five-sided cone (a pentagon, not a star) and a star radius of 0.14 of the length. The two flag materials cost 2 draw calls.
