# Ho Chi Minh City improvements

Source: `src/cities/list/ho-chi-minh.ts`. Part of the [city improvements](README.md) audit.

## Status

- **Budget now:** 86,616 tris, 159 draws (limits 90,000 / 220). The motorbikes take about 33k tris (48 riders on `motorbike-lite`, 696 tris each). Pedestrians cost ~208 tris each since the `person()` rework; 40 are placed.
- **Built 2026-10-21 from `assets/ho-chi-minh.png`:** positions read through a homography fitted to the poster's plinth corners, streets, river and plazas traced on a rectified top-down copy of its ground plane. The Saigon River runs along the left edge and stays inside the plinth: the water stops behind a stone curb along the plinth's top edge (`RIM`), so the plinth sides show no water. The poster's water-in-section sides were dropped at the user's request.
  - **Ba Son bridge:** built as the real bridge rather than the poster's two A-frame pylons: one arch-shaped pylon in the river by the far bank, leaning back away from the main span, 14 stays fanned to both deck edges and 6 backstays. The deck lands on the city bank and ramps down behind the Independence Palace; traffic on the bridge follows the deck height and pitch (`onDeck`). Pylon, stays and parapet strips are lit at night, like the real bridge's 2024 "harp of light".
  - **Landmark 81:** nine bundled square tubes stepping up to the centre, a two-stage crown, spire and aviation light; floor bands glow at night.
  - **Bitexco:** a custom lotus-bud shaft (elliptical floors that swell and taper), a slanted crown rising away from the helipad, the helipad cantilevered toward the river, floor fins and two dark belts. Pale steel blue by day, blue at night. Refined 2026-10-21 from the reference render `.rodin/refs/biteco.png` (kept procedural; Rodin would lose the glazing grid and the night glow): a three-tier podium of rounded terraces with glazed bands and pale lips, a pale spine up the helipad side, a lip round the slanted cut, and mullions on every other column (two floors per segment, about 1.5k tris in all). `.rodin/refs/landmark.png` matched the existing Landmark 81, which was left as is.
  - **Independence Palace:** the stone-bamboo fin screen on every front, pilotis, roof canopy, rooftop pavilion with the flag, and the helicopter. Its lawn and curved drive face the oval park, whose fountain and flag sit inside a traffic ring.
  - **Notre-Dame Cathedral, Opera House, Central Post Office, Ben Thanh Market:** brick bell towers with belfries, grey spires, rose windows, transept and apse; a mansard hall with the great entrance arch, paired columns and statues; yellow wings of arched windows around the clock gable; arcaded halls, corner pavilions and the three-faced clock tower over the red name band. All floodlit at night.
  - **Streets:** shophouse rows (shops in front, a taller row behind), three apartment blocks, the night-market stalls under a lantern string, the palm plaza monument, palms along the avenues and the promenade.
  - **Ben Thanh square and crossroads (today's layout, at the user's choice):** the poster's roundabout is gone, as the real one has been since 2017, when it made way for the Ben Thanh metro station. The avenue (1.6 wide, narrowed from 2.2 to make room) runs straight past a granite-paved square in front of the south gate: lawns either side of the central walk with small shade trees and flower beds, Tran Nguyen Han on horseback (bronze, on a stepped granite pedestal) back on his old spot facing the gate, and the white Quach Thi Trang bust among the trees on the east lawn, as the 2025–2026 renovation lays it out. The junction at the market's front-west corner (the avenue and the street past the cathedral) is a signalised crossroads: a light on each corner, red for the avenue and green for the cross street, crosswalks on all four arms, and a crossing from the square on the gate's axis.
  - **Traffic:** each pack is one mover, a car or taxi with scooters or a swarm of up to six scooters, on lanes that keep right: closed loops round the oval park, Ben Thanh and the Landmark 81 block, and open paths that end a pack's length inside the plinth edge. The avenue's lanes are westbound 7.8 (curb, the Ben Thanh loop) and 8.15, eastbound 8.8, all clear of the square. Riders wear helmets (the baked hat region painted in helmet colours); shirts and scooter bodies come from one palette, so a pack costs 5–7 draws. A cargo ship and a waterbus run the river; a dinner cruise and sampans lie at the promenade.
- **Rodin:** reuses the Hanoi scooter as `motorbike-lite`, a 700-triangle bake of `.rodin/motorbike.glb` through the manifest's `from` field ([rodin-pipeline.md](../rodin-pipeline.md)). No new reference images.
- **Left open:** packs on different paths still pass through each other where they share a lane (V1 northbound). The traffic lights are static, so traffic does not stop at them. Open paths end at ±9.0, so packs appear and vanish about a unit inside the plinth edge. The poster's density (~100 scooters) is out of budget; triangle headroom is ~3.4k, so new detail needs triangles freed elsewhere first (e.g. fewer riders). The Bitexco podium is mostly hidden behind the apartments and shophouses from the default camera.
