### Landmark fidelity
- **Golden Gate Bridge** (`goldenGateBridge()`): good overall (Deco towers, struts, suspenders, orange). Gaps: (1) bridge fills the whole front edge and hides most of the bay and Alcatraz from the default camera; the real bridge opens onto the bay, with the city behind. Consider angling it (rotate ry ~ -0.25) or lowering the deck. (2) No night lighting: deck lamps/tower beacons not lit (`lampMaterial` unused on bridge, night shot shows dark red bridge). Add 2 lamp rows on the deck and beacons on tower tops. (3) Side-span cables end at anchorages at y 1.35 with no pylons/saddles; fine. (4) Missing fog [unverified, see sparse].
- **Alcatraz** (`alcatraz()`): too small (≈1.7 units ≈ 60px on screen) and mostly hidden behind the bridge cables; the cellhouse, water tower and lighthouse are all there but tiny. Enlarge ×1.4 and move toward the clear water at x≈-3, z≈5.4 (outside the bridge sight line) so it reads.
- **Transamerica Pyramid** (`transamerica()`): proportions and wings are right, stripes ok. Gap: it is mostly white with flat window bands; real pyramid is white quartz with thin dark glass slits; reduce band height or tint bands light grey. Stands 9.4 + 2.3 spire = 11.7 (taller than Salesforce visually, real is 260m vs 326m). Salesforce is 11.8 and Transamerica 11.7 ⇒ swap: Salesforce ≈ 12.6 total, Transamerica ≈ 9.8 (H 7.8). Also pyramid base footprint (1.7 box) is much larger than real; reduce.
- **Salesforce Tower** (`salesforceTower()`): lathe silhouette is good (blunt cone, rounded crown). Gap: the crown lacks the openwork top and the horizontal bands are heavy (cylinder rings every 0.45); lighten to ~30% fewer rings (saves tris, not draws).
- **Coit Tower** (`coitTower()`): correct fluted column and lantern, but only ≈2.1 tall on a 1.55 mound, ~60px; mound (`build()` line 394) is a bare smooth green cone, reads as a lampshade. Break the cone with 2-3 terraces/stairs, a ring road, and enlarge the tower ×1.3.
- **Painted Ladies** (`victorian()` x6): very good (bay, gable, porch). Gap: all six have identical massing; vary heights/roof pitch, add a corner turret on the 1st and 6th, and add a second row of cheaper block houses behind to show the dense Victorian rows.
- **Row houses on the steep street** (`build()` 361-373): fine; identical gables, no chimneys difference; podium wall colour `0xa69f94` repeated. Add 2 houses in line so the street reads as long (e.g. z 3.5 & -3.5) and use stair-stepping garages.
- **Downtown filler** (`downtownTower()` + array line 326): all share one `tower()` with `bands` and 3 crowns; feels repetitive, but spread is fine. Give 2 of them a different top: 555 California (dark slab with notch), Bank of America-style box with sloped glass.
### Missing / sparse
- No Bay Bridge, Ferry Building, piers/Fisherman's Wharf, Lombard Street, Palace of Fine Arts, Sutro Tower, Oracle Park. Cheapest wins: Ferry Building with clock tower (~25 tris) at the water/city seam (x≈2, z≈4.3) and a pier row in the bay at x≈2..6, z≈4.5..6 (reuses `M.wood` and existing boats, 1-2 draws).
- Hill relief: the city is a flat plateau; real SF is hills. Telegraph Hill is the only elevation. Raise the downtown block into a low terrace or add a Twin Peaks / Sutro Tower hill at the back-left corner (-9,-9.5) with a three-leg red/white mast (`beam`).
- The fog signature is missing. Option: low translucent fog strips around bridge towers (a single transparent `patch` with `mat(..., {opacity})`, 1 draw). [unverified whether kit materials support opacity]
- Street life: no pedestrians downtown (only 7 persons in Alamo park); no cable car on a second line; Cable car (`cableCar()`) is a single tiny 0.3-wide object with 8px detail; add a second car that runs in opposition and a traffic-light stub at the 4-way (-0.6, 3.4).
- Back-right corner (x 3..9, z -9.6..-8.1 behind the ladies) and front-right (x 3..9, z 1..4) are plain grey `LAND` slabs with nothing on them: a visible empty paving area between the cable-car street and Coit hill (screenshot crop: large blank grey). Fill with blocks of low houses or a park.
- **Draw-call headroom is the constraint** (198/220). `car()` pre-merges per car into ~4 draws (paint, glass, lamp, wheels; kit `car()` lines 482-494), so the 24 animated cars cost ≈90-100 draws (4+4+3+3+2 city, 8 on the bridge) and cannot be batched across instances. Also each `mat(wallc)` per house and per tower colour (`PASTEL` ×6, `TRIMS` ×6, tower colours ×9, plus per-call `mat(0x484d55)` etc.) is a separate draw. To free room: cut bridge cars to 4, city cars to ~10 and turn a lane into a chain of 2 cars welded in one group (one `car` pair per `anim.follow`), or let a shared helper cache `mat` colours (use the PASTEL colours for towers). Count of freed draws ≈ 30-40 → pays for Ferry Building, piers, second cable car.
### Rodin candidates
- None worth it now: forms are architecture/bridge/lattice (procedural). Only candidate: Alcatraz island / Telegraph Hill rock terrain **if** they are enlarged to ≥120px (cliffs, ledges, vegetation) — today both are built from stacked cylinders; an organic `shape()` for Telegraph Hill would fix the "lampshade" look; Alcatraz at current size is < 120px so no.
