# Cairo improvements

Source: `src/cities/list/cairo.ts`. Part of the [city improvements](README.md) audit of 2026-10-21.

## Status

- **Budget now:** 56,042 tris, 158 draws (limits 200,000 / 300), measured 2026-10-23 after the kit tree rework.
- **Fixed 2026-10-21:** Open hyperboloid Cairo Tower with lit cabin; mosque half-domes and lit drum; overlaps; dunes.
- **Fixed 2026-10-21, second pass:** Ground: darker warm `SAND` floor, raised (0.12) `ROCK` Giza plateau polygon (one `area` replaces the two overlapping `DESERT` patches) carrying dunes, pyramids, Sphinx, plateau camel and visitors; lighter `PAVE` plazas (bridgehead, Islamic Cairo, Old Cairo) and dusty `STREET` lanes (z=0.3, x=3.45) in Old Cairo. Sphinx now sits in a sand-coloured elliptical pit (no orange slab) and its stone is `floodMat`-floodlit at night. Qasr el-Nil bridge at x=-1.2 (hump deck `profile` with 3 arches, parapets, 4 lion pedestals, 4 lamps); felucca lanes split into four one-boat lanes from the bridge to the plinth edges (route-around), corniche palm at x=-1.88 dropped. Streetlamps along the corniche (z=4.76, 9) and around the Sphinx pit (4). `cairoHouse()`: mashrabiya bays with `windowMaterial` slits, arched doors/windows on Old Cairo blocks, rooftop tanks. Lotus crown enlarged (petals r 0.8, 1.55 tall, bigger lit core). Citadel: rim battlements, gate tower with lit slit and arched portal; minaret finials with `lampMaterial` tips. Mid-block crosswalks removed; two Old Cairo houses nudged off the plateau edge. `muhammadAli()` body/domes/courtyard untouched (Rodin replacement pending).
- **Left open:** boats pop in under the bridge deck (sails show above it); camel caravan, buses and moving pedestrians; ablaq cornice stripes and satellite dishes; pyramid band variety; tower-island quay/ferry; Cairo Tower and pyramids have no night lighting beyond the lotus core. Scatter positions shifted again (people now placed on the plateau).
- **Fixed 2026-10-22:** the procedural mosque body, domes and courtyard in `muhammadAli()` are replaced by the Rodin shape `muhammad-ali-mosque` (2,996 tris, parts lead domes + alabaster walls, `height` 2.3, courtyard toward +z, spanning z -2.43..1.83 inside the citadel, clear of the gate tower). The walls use `MOSQUE_WALL`, a `floodMat` that floodlights them at night; the two minarets moved to the courtyard's front corners (x ±1.5, z 1.8). Citadel plateau, towers, battlements, gate tower and fortress buildings are unchanged. The baked mosque has no lit drum windows.
- **Next:** Add a camel caravan on `anim.follow` (draw budget has room); vary felucca tints; lit crescent finials on the mosque domes.

## Audit

Original findings, written before the 2026-10-21 fix pass; line numbers refer to the code at that date. Reference: none (compared with real Giza / Citadel / Cairo Tower). Budget at audit time: 31904 tris, 103 draws (headroom ≈58k tris, ≈117 draws; the most generous of all cities).

Checked on screenshots (day + night, zoomed crops); no console errors.

### Bugs
- **Old Cairo house collides with the Citadel's front-right round tower** (`build()` `oldCairo` entry `[9.0, -3.0, 1.3, 1.6, 0.7]` vs `muhammadAli()` corner tower at world (6.0+3.3, -6.2+3.0) = (9.3, -3.2), plateau front edge z=-3.2): the house spans x 8.35–9.65, z -3.8…-2.2, so it swallows the tower base and cuts into the plateau wall (visible bottom-right of the citadel, day and night). Move it to ~(9.0, -1.6) / drop it, or shrink to w 0.9 at z ≥ -2.5.
- **Khafre overhangs the plinth** (`pyramid(3.8, …, -8.0, -3.0, 12, CASING)`): the base slab `cylinder(R*1.02…)` reaches x ≈ -9.94, beyond the documented ±9.8 limit. Move to x ≈ -7.8 (or shrink w to 3.6).
- **Khufu and Khafre bases interpenetrate** (`pyramid(4.4,…,-4.6,-5.6)` x -6.8…-2.4, z -7.8…-3.4 vs Khafre x -9.9…-6.1, z -4.9…-0.9): overlap x -6.8…-6.1, z -4.9…-3.4. Reads as Khafre's right corner running into Khufu's left face (c3 crop). Push Khafre to z ≈ -2.4 / x ≈ -8.3 (diagonal layout, as in reality) so the bases just touch.
- **Felucca lanes clip the Gezira island vegetation** (`lanes` z=2.15 and z=3.9; island `patch(4.6,1.5,…, z=NZ)` = z 2.25–3.75, round trees at z 2.45, palms `s:1.3` at z 3.55): hulls pass 0.1–0.15 from the island edge, so sails/hulls go through palm fronds (observed at the tower island, day + night). Move lanes to z≈1.95 and z≈4.05, or thin/shorten the island rows.
- **Thin desert "dune" strips are invisible dead geometry** (`for (i<6) patch(3+rand*2, 0.08, …, h:0.05)`): 0.08 deep slivers show nothing on screen. Replace with real low dunes (squashed `dome`/`lathe` mounds) behind/left of the pyramids, or delete.
- **Overlapping same-material slabs** (`patch(10.4,7.2,DESERT)` x -9.8…0.6, z -8.8…-1.6 vs `patch(5.6,4.2,DESERT)` x -9.8…-4.2, z -2.9…1.3 overlap x -9.8…-4.2, z -2.9…-1.6; same y/h). Same material so no visible z-fight, but wasteful: make one `area([...])` L-shape or offset heights.
- **Dead geometry in `cairoTower()`**: the 8 lotus-petal `beam`s (y top+1.1…top+1.75, r 0.5→0.1) sit inside the solid `lathe` bulb (r 0.45–0.5, y top+0.92…+1.77), so they are never visible (see tower crown in crops).

### Landmark fidelity
- **Cairo Tower** (`cairoTower()`): real tower is an open hyperboloid lattice (diamond mesh, waisted, slightly flared), a cylindrical cabin, then an open lotus-bud lattice. Here: a tapering solid core with a mottled stripe lattice (reads as brown bark), and a *solid white onion dome* crown with a blue band. → Make the shaft waisted (`radius(y)` with a minimum at ~60% height, then flare), drop the solid core to a thin inner shaft so diamonds are see-through, add 3 rings of crossing `beam`s (both diagonal directions are already generated; widen `cols` to 12–16), replace the `lathe` bulb with the 8 open petal beams (+ ring), keep a small closed cabin. Tower is ~140 px tall on screen and the main vertical hero, so it deserves this; budget is ample (~+800 tris).
- **Muhammad Ali Mosque** (`muhammadAli()`): central dome + 4 half-domes + 4 corner domes + two pencil minarets flanking the courtyard is the right program, but the four "half" domes are full `dome(0.78…)` spheres, so the roof reads as a cloud of grey bubbles, not the stepped cascade. No drum windows, arches or fountain canopy. The citadel is a bare orange box with 4 stub cylinders. → Use real half-domes (hemisphere clipped by the main wall; e.g. `lathe` quarter profile + `ry`), put each one lower than the central dome and drop the corner domes to y+1.3 drums with 8 windows; add a drum of windowMaterial slits under the central dome (lit at night); give the courtyard a proper arcade (it is three 0.2-thick walls) and a central domed ablution fountain on 8 columns (present but a plain slab); add crenellations/battlements along the citadel wall (`box` row on the 0.12 lip at y 0.9) and a gate tower facing +z. Minarets: add 2 balconies + lead cone (exists) — their height 4.0 over the mosque is good.
- **Pyramids** (`pyramid()`): silhouette/proportions OK (Khafre with white `CASING` cap is a good touch), but the alternating light/dark bands read as layer-cake rather than blocky masonry; Khufu has no capstone/entrance and no sense of the rough eroded core. → Alternate band colours less regularly (3 tones keyed to `rand`), add a missing-casing patch / dark entrance notch on the +z face, make Menkaure's lowest courses darker (red granite).
- **Sphinx** (`sphinx()`): baked Rodin shape reads well (nemes headdress and paws visible, ~160 px). Scale is ~2.5× real relative to the pyramids (1.6 vs 4.4; real ≈ 0.14) which is fine for readability, but it sits on an unnatural bright-orange rectangular slab (`box(3.9,0.05,1.6)`) that does not match the pale ground; → sink it in a sand-coloured shallow pit (area with `STONE_DARK`, rounded outline, y 0.03) and add the two small temple ruins/steps in front.
- **Camels** (`camel()`): ~25 px tall, tiny and rigid but readable as red-blanketed camels. Two stand at the quay edge facing nothing. → make them walk a caravan (`anim.follow` on a path from the Sphinx towards the pyramids, `mergeStatic` first) and group 4–5 with one rider (`person`).
- **Houses** (`cairoHouse()`): modern "Western" shopfronts (grid windows, coloured awnings, rooftop cube, grey flat roofs) — they do not say Islamic Cairo. → Add mashrabiya bay (a box projecting 0.08 at upper floor, brown, `windowMaterial` behind), arched ground-floor windows via `profile`/`archHole`, striped (ablaq) cornice band, water tanks + satellite dishes on roofs; keep ≤0.3k tris each. Old Cairo blocks (no `shop`) are flat boxes with a brown slab + glowing slit on +z only (looks like a bunker front).
- **Felucca** (`felucca()`): Rodin hull + lateen sail reads correctly, but ~30 px and all four are identical colour; vary hull/sail tint per boat (second `mat`s) so they read as a fleet.

### Missing / sparse
- **No bridge across the Nile.** The river is a cut channel, the two banks are unconnected; Qasr el-Nil Bridge (flat arched deck + 4 lions on pedestals) would link the corniche to the island and the Sphinx plaza. Add procedural: `profile` span with 3 `archHole`s at x≈-2…-1 (front of the tower, clear of its sight line), plus 4 lion pedestals (tiny `box` + `sphere`). ~1k tris, ~3 draws.
- **Tower island is bare**: a 4.6×1.5 green patch with one paved slab; add a small Opera-house-like/palm-lined quay, benches, and a path; and a second river vessel type (Nile cruise ship/ dahabiya / ferry) at the front lane so the water is not only tiny sails.
- **Ground is all one cream colour** (`SAND` 0xe9d3a3 renders ≈ pale cream; `DESERT` 0xeedcb0 even lighter): Giza plateau, plazas, pavements and the Old Cairo streets are indistinguishable, so landmarks lack grounding and the "desert" is missing. → Darken/warm `SAND` and `DESERT`, give Giza a distinct rocky plateau (`PLATEAU`-ish raised 0.12 slab) with dunes + a few palm clumps, keep paved plazas lighter. Old Cairo has no streets at all (back-right is just boxes on cream).
- **Empty zones**: strip x -9.8…-6 / z -2…1 (left of the Sphinx), the whole x -6…-1, z -1.5…1.3 plaza (only 7 microscopic people), the area between Old Cairo and the river (z 0…1.4), and the north corner behind the pyramids.
- **Street life**: only 10 cars (all identical taxis) and ~20 static people. No donkey carts, tuk-tuks, microbuses (`bus` exists), no pedestrians moving, no camel caravan, no flags/market canopies. Add 2 buses and a few animated pedestrians on `anim.follow`.
- **No streetlamps at all** (`lamp` is never used): the corniche is dark at night. Add `row(…lamp…)` along z≈4.75 and around the Sphinx plaza.
- **Night look**: houses and the mosque slits glow, but the pyramids/Sphinx/tower get no lighting; the Cairo Tower glazing uses a plain metal `mat`, not `windowMaterial`/`lampMaterial`. Make the deck glazing `windowMaterial`, add a lit lotus crown, minaret finials/crescents with `lampMaterial`, and a faint warm glow strip at the pyramid bases (Giza sound-and-light show) — cheap `lampMaterial` boxes.
- **Crosswalks** at `crosswalk(-6.8,5.2…)` / `(4.0,5.2…)` sit mid-block with no side street; ok visually but pointless — align with side-street junctions or remove.

### Rodin candidates
- None worth adding. The organic shapes (Sphinx, camel, felucca) are already baked. Candidates only if enlarged: a Nile cruise boat / dahabiya (organic hull + sail, ~60–80 px at current size → probably too small, `[unverified]`), and a donkey cart. The Cairo Tower, minarets, pyramids and mosque must stay procedural (lattice/architecture).

### Priority
1. Cairo Tower rework (open waisted hyperboloid lattice + real lotus crown, lit deck at night) and the mosque's stepped half-dome cascade; these two are the vertical heroes and currently read as "brown stick with a white dome" and "grey bubbles".
2. Fix the bugs: Old Cairo house vs citadel tower (`[9.0,-3.0]`), Khafre plinth overhang / Khufu overlap, felucca lanes vs island palms; delete or replace the invisible dune strips. Then differentiate ground colour (Giza desert vs paved plazas vs streets).
3. Add Qasr el-Nil bridge with lions plus Cairo-flavoured houses (mashrabiya, arches) and street life/lamps (camel caravan, buses, lamps) — fills the empty plaza and gives the night a lit corniche.
