# Sydney improvements

Source: `src/cities/list/sydney.ts`. Part of the [city improvements](README.md) audit of 2026-10-21.

## Status

- **Fixed 2026-10-21:** Opera glazing and mullions, bridge lamps, tower beacon, clock hall, lawn gaps, rib aliasing.
- **Fixed 2026-10-21 (second pass):** `BEACON` is now `floodMat(0xd9d4cc, 0xff2211, 2.5)`: a pale lens by day, red glow at night only. `cbdTower()` gained crown types 3 (glass pyramid with spire), 4 (stepped Art-Deco with flagpole) and 5 (slanted wedge), spread over 11 towers in `CBD`. North Shore now holds `lunaPark()` (striped twin towers, lit grin, ferris-wheel ring) at (4.9, 8.65), two low apartment blocks (3.2, 9.1) and (6.3, 9.0) with a shopfront, gable / mansard / hip houses, a Kirribilli ferry wharf (`wharf()` turned and shortened) with a moored ferry at (5.2, 7.6), and `fortDenison()` (round sandstone tower, slate cap, lantern) at (0.3, 8.2). The Luna Park face was not inspected zoomed on screen (the orbit camera could not be framed on the front corner); the wharf, houses, fort and Walsh Bay were checked by day and night. No Rocks interior terraces or extra cross street yet.
- **Next:** Denser Rocks interior (second cross street, smaller terraces, `rocksHouse` dormers or awnings); a longer liner at the Walsh Bay terminal; a seaplane or ferry wakes if draws allow.

## Audit

Original findings, written before the 2026-10-21 fix pass; line numbers refer to the code at that date. Reference: assets/sydney.jpeg. Budget at audit time: 53591 tris, 177 draws (headroom ~36k tris, ~43 draws).

Observed: day + night full shots and a zoomed day shot (cropped Opera House, Quay, bridge/Rocks, garden); `tab.errors()` empty.

### Bugs
- No visible z-fighting, floating or sunk objects in the day or night shots. The AABB lead (land vs paving/lawn at y 0.05) is mostly a false positive here: `area(water,{h:0.05})` abuts the paving patches edge to edge. Minor real coplanar overlaps remain (top faces equal, different materials), none flickering in stills:
  - `build()` L636 forecourt `patch(1.2,5.0,PAVING,{h:0.05})` spans x -4.75..-3.55 and overlaps the water polygon edge (`OPERA_EAST` x≈-3.6) and the garden road end (L654 `road(...,-4.85,...)` top 0.05, same height as the patch, 0.1 overlap). [unverified in motion] Fix: end the road at x=-4.75, or make the patch `h:0.055`.
  - L691-693 lawns: `patch(2.0,6.2,...x:4,z:-6.6)` covers z -9.7..-3.5 and the `area` starts at z -3.4, leaving a 0.1 gap. `patch(1.0,6.1,x:5.7)` starts at x 5.2 while the first ends at x 5.0, leaving a 0.2 strip. The paving cap shows through as a grey line. Fix: widen the first patch to z -3.3 and merge the three lawns into one `area`.
- Opera shell rib bands are aliased: `shell()` L106 colours whole quads with `Math.floor((j+|k-K/2+.5|)*.5)%4`, so the cream chevrons render as jagged stair-step blotches and look dirty at zoom (visible in the Opera crop). Fix: raise `S`/`K` (~28/28), or lay ribs as thin raised `tube()`/strip geometry along the meridians instead of per-quad colour. Spend ≤1.5k tris.
- Clock tower (`clockHall()` L425-434): black `M.dark` belfry box (y 2.3, 0.64 wide) sits directly above the clock discs and reads as black holes beside the clock faces. The 0.16 clocks are tiny next to the 0.62 shaft. Fix: use `CREAM`/louvre-coloured belfry with slim dark slits, and enlarge the clock discs to ~0.22.
- Night: the Opera House sails are not lit, the bronze glazing in `shell()` L130 uses `BRONZE`, not `windowMaterial`, and the podium foyer band is only 0.08 high. The Harbour Bridge has no deck lamps or pylon floodlights, and the harbour reads almost black-navy (`WATER`).

### Landmark fidelity
- **Opera House** (`operaHouse()` L150, `shell()` L85): the silhouette is right (two halls, 4+3 sails, steps, podium) and the placement matches the poster. Gaps: sails are uniformly semi-circular vaults with the same profile, whereas the real ones are tilted sail-like shells with pointed tops and a leaning back. The glazed ends are a flat brown `ShapeGeometry`, with no mullions. Fix: add 5-6 vertical mullion `beam`s plus a transom per glass wall, and switch the glass to a lit `windowMaterial` tinted bronze. The shells are 0.88-scaled and the podium is wider than the poster's, with a large empty paved forecourt (`patch` L636). Add a Forecourt/Bennelong lawn edge and trees, or shrink the patch.
- **Harbour Bridge** (`harbourBridge()` L215, `pylon()` L198): the through-arch with Warren web, hangers and approach viaduct is faithful, and the road+rail deck reads. Gaps: no pylon-top flag masts or the "SYDNEY" lamps. The southern approach is a plain slab, while the poster shows the Rocks houses butting up to the Cahill Expressway/Argyle. The four pylon pairs are separate granite stacks instead of a single full-width pylon with arch openings on each side, which is what the poster shows (a chunky pylon at the bearing). Fix: widen `pylon()` to span the deck, add the arch hole on the longitudinal face, and add 8 deck lamp posts (`lamp()` along L734 lane edges, 0.6 spacing).
- **Sydney Tower** (`sydneyTower()` L365): excellent (gold flared turret, guy cables, spike), tallest item, matches the poster. Gap: the shaft is a single constant-taper cylinder; add the 3 small pod bands lower down and a coloured (red/white) aviation beacon with `lampMaterial` for night.
- **CBD** (`CBD` L561, `cbdTower()` L316): 19 grid towers + `roundTower()` (Australia Square) in a pleasing mix, but every tower is a plain box with the same 3 crown types. The poster's skyline has distinct tops: tapering/chamfered crowns, a glass-prism tower, a stepped Art-Deco top, the tall round tower with a roof ring. Fix: add `top` variants 3 (chamfered `prism`) and 4 (stepped setback + flagpole), and give the 2-3 tallest towers a wedge/slanted roof. Also vary footprint with an L-shaped `prism` for 2 blocks.
- **Circular Quay / station** (`quayStation()` L486, `wharf()` L476): the lit glazed concourse, platform canopy and viaduct read well. Gap: the elevated railway does not continue to the bridge. In the poster the line runs along the water behind the finger wharves to the bridge pylons and the Cahill Expressway runs above. Add a viaduct segment from x≈-0.8 to the bridge `BRIDGE_X-1.5` (z≈-3.0, `quayStation()` params can't carry it: write a `viaduct()` helper using the deck+bents code from L270-281).
- **Clock hall / Rocks** (`clockHall()` L409, `rocksHouse()` L438): a decent GPO/Customs House stand-in, and the sandstone/brick/terracotta mix matches the poster palette. Gap: `rocksHouse` is a single generic template (same sash grid, same hip roofs). Add per-house variation: attic dormers, chimneys (`building` already supports them on pitched roofs), verandah/awning (`shop:`), a corner-turret on one house, and colonial two-tone trim.
- **Walsh Bay / Overseas Passenger Terminal** (`terminal()` L443, L699-709): the terminal reads as a blue-grey shed but is bland. In the poster the OPT is a long curved-roof shed with a cruise ship alongside. The liner (`boat({len:1.5})` L706) is tiny and sits 0.28 off the edge, barely visible. Fix: a proper 2.4-long ship with stacked decks (4 `box` tiers + funnel) at the OPT wharf, extending past the pier.
- **Botanic Garden** (L600-633): reads as a generic park (1 pond, 1 path network, ~37 trees). Missing Government House, Farm Cove sea-wall steps and the Mrs Macquarie's Chair point. Add a small turreted sandstone house (`building` hip + corner tower) in the garden at ~(-8.3, 8.4) and a bandstand/rotunda or fountain.

### Missing / sparse
- No Fort Denison or other harbour island; no Luna Park / Kirribilli on the North Shore (`NORTH_SHORE` L544 holds only 2 houses at L713-714 and tree rows). Add a small sandstone fort on `cylinder` + `cone` at ~(0.5, 6.2) (harbour is empty there, 3 sailboats only) and a Luna Park face (`box` + 2 `cylinder` towers, `lampMaterial` lights) at ~(4.5, 8.8).
- North Shore (front corner, the most visible area, ~9×3 units) is mostly lawn with 2 houses and trees; adding 5-6 low houses/apartment blocks and a ferry wharf would fill it. Right-side Walsh Bay is a cluster of 4 flat-roofed warehouses (L699-702) that look repetitive (same `rocksHouse` flat top). Add gable/hip variants and chimneys/gantry cranes.
- Rocks interior (x -0.5..2.0, z -6..-2) is sparse; the poster shows dense terraces. Cobble `road` L680 is a single street; add a second cross street and 4-5 smaller terraces with shared walls.
- Street life: `CBD traffic` L749-753 is 4 cars + 1 taxi on the garden road and a few people; most CBD streets are empty or hidden between towers (`road` L655-659, L656 are unobstructed but not seen). Add 4-6 buses/cars on the quay promenade and Rocks cobble road, and a ferry arriving/departing a wharf (only `ferryLoop` L722 boats sail; the two moored ferries L646-647 are static).
- Animation gaps: no ferry wakes or wake foam, no helicopter/seaplane; add a seaplane `Animator.follow` over the harbour and white-foam trail (`box` strip, additive) behind ferries.
- Night: CBD windows and street/boat lights are good (`windowMaterial` lit towers read as the poster's night), but Opera sails, bridge, pylons, Fort/station canopy and the Sydney Tower beacon stay dark. Add bridge deck lamps and arch obstruction lights (`lampMaterial`), a sail uplight (`windowMaterial` strip at the base of each shell group) and a lit-water tint for `WATER`.

### Rodin candidates
- None. The only organic forms are the Opera House sails, which are already modelled procedurally and look correct, trees/people at <120px, and a harbour island (small). Rodin is not warranted; invest in the procedural fixes above.
